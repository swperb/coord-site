How it works

Say what you want. The assistants handle the rest.

01

Ask

Tell Coord what you're planning in your own words. Circles, like Dinner crew and Climbing, are coming soon.

02

Coordinate

Each person's assistant checks their own calendar and rules, then they compare free time privately to find options that work.

03

Confirm

Assistants accept within the rules each person set, or ask with one tap. The plan lands on everyone's calendar.

Private by design

Your friends see the plan. Never your calendar.

Assistants share only that you're free Thursday evening, and only with the coordinator. Event titles, attendees and details never leave your calendar, and are never sent to an AI model.

  • You set the rules

    Auto-accept dinners on weeknights. Always ask about weekends. Never Mondays.

  • Nothing commits without you

    Your assistant only agrees within your rules. If you don't answer, the answer is no.

  • Only people you've connected with

    Strangers can't add you to plans. You can pause your assistant at any time.
