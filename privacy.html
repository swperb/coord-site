Privacy

Effective October 26, 2026

This policy covers this website, the demo at demo/, and the waitlist. The Coord app will have its own policy before it opens to anyone.

Who runs Coord

Coord is run by Jay Proctor, an individual, who is the controller of the information described here. Contact: stephenproctor291@gmail.com.

What we collect

Only the email address you enter in the waitlist form.

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics, ads or tracking. The demo runs in your browser on sample data and sends nothing anywhere.

How we use it

To email you about early access to Coord and its launch. We don't sell or rent it, share it for advertising, or use it to train AI models.

Our legal basis under the GDPR is your consent. You can withdraw it at any time, and every email we send will have a way to unsubscribe.

Who else handles it

  • Tally (Tally BV, Belgium) runs the waitlist form and stores submissions on servers in the European Union for us. The form loads from tally.so only when you open it. Tally's privacy policy.
  • GitHub (GitHub, Inc., USA) hosts this site. Like any web host, it receives your IP address and browser details when you load a page, and keeps them in security logs. GitHub's privacy statement.

We will use an email service to send waitlist emails, and will name it here before the first one goes out. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Until you ask us to remove it, or until 12 months after Coord opens to the public, whichever comes first.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your information, or object to how we use it. Write to the contact address above and we'll respond within 30 days. If you're in the EU, UK or EEA, you can also complain to your data protection authority.

We don't sell or share personal information as California law defines those terms. Because nothing on this site tracks you, Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals need no further action.

Children

Coord isn't for anyone under 16. If we learn we've collected a child's email, we delete it.

Security

The site is served only over HTTPS. Submissions are stored with Tally, not on this site.

Changes

We'll post changes here and update the date at the top. If a change affects how we use your email, we'll tell you by email first.
